Optimize Prompts for Enzymatic Research

Use specialized prompts to get the most value from AI assistants for your enzymatic allergen research.

Enzymatic Research Prompt Library:

📄 Paper Analysis Prompts
🔬 Comprehensive Enzymatic Paper Analysis
Analyze this enzymatic cleaning research paper focusing on: 1. Enzyme type and source (subtilisin, papain, recombinant, etc.) 2. Experimental parameters (concentration, contact time, pH, temperature) 3. Surface materials tested (stainless steel, plastic, etc.) 4. Allergen type and detection method (ELISA type, LOD, LOQ) 5. Effectiveness metrics (% removal, residual allergen levels) 6. Comparison to control methods (chemical cleaning, etc.) 7. Cost considerations mentioned 8. Limitations and future research directions Present findings in a structured table and suggest 2-3 follow-up experiments.
📊 Data Extraction from Tables/Figures
Extract all quantitative data from tables and figures in this paper related to: - Enzyme concentration vs. effectiveness - Contact time vs. allergen removal - pH/temperature optimization data - Control vs. experimental comparisons - Statistical significance values Organize extracted data into a CSV-like format. Identify trends and optimal conditions.
🔍 Literature Review Prompts
🌐 Comparative Analysis of Multiple Papers
Compare these 5 papers on enzymatic allergen removal: 1. Create a comparison table with columns: Author/Year, Enzyme Used, Concentration, Contact Time, Surface, Effectiveness, Validation Method, Cost Mentioned 2. Identify trends across studies (e.g., optimal conditions, most effective enzymes) 3. Note contradictions or inconsistent findings 4. Identify research gaps in the collective literature 5. Suggest a synthesis experiment that builds on these studies Focus specifically on practical applications for food factory settings.
📈 Trend Analysis in Enzymatic Research
Analyze the evolution of enzymatic cleaning research from 2015-2025: 1. Identify shifts in focus (e.g., from laboratory to industrial applications) 2. Note changes in preferred enzymes over time 3. Track improvements in detection methods (ELISA sensitivity) 4. Identify emerging technologies (sprayable formulations, immobilized enzymes) 5. Predict next 5-year trends based on current trajectory Provide specific citations for each trend identified.
💡 Research Design Prompts
🧪 Experiment Design for Sprayable Enzymes
Design an experiment to test a sprayable subtilisin formulation for peanut allergen removal: 1. Hypothesis: Based on existing literature 2. Materials: Specific enzymes, surfaces, allergens, detection methods 3. Methods: Detailed protocol including concentrations, contact times, controls 4. Expected outcomes: Predicted effectiveness ranges 5. Potential pitfalls: Common issues in enzymatic cleaning experiments 6. Validation methods: ELISA protocols, swabbing techniques 7. Statistical analysis plan Include cost estimates for materials and equipment.
🎯 Gap Analysis and Research Questions
Based on this literature, identify specific research gaps in enzymatic allergen removal: 1. Unexplored enzyme combinations or formulations 2. Missing surface materials (specific plastics, textured surfaces) 3. Lack of long-term stability data 4. Absence of cost-benefit analyses for SMEs 5. Regulatory pathway uncertainties 6. Unaddressed scale-up challenges For each gap, formulate a specific research question and suggest a feasible experiment.
📝 Writing and Proposal Prompts
✍️ Research Proposal Drafting
Help me draft a research proposal on "Development of a sprayable enzyme formulation for peanut allergen removal": 1. Introduction: Context and literature review (cite 5-7 key papers) 2. Research gap: Specific problem this addresses 3. Objectives: 3-4 specific, measurable aims 4. Methodology: Detailed experimental design 5. Expected outcomes and significance 6. Timeline and milestones 7. Budget justification Emphasize practical applications for food industry and alignment with FDA/EU regulations.

Prompt Engineering Tips for Enzymatic Research:

  1. Be specific: Include enzyme names, concentrations, surfaces
  2. Request structure: Ask for tables, bullet points, or specific formats
  3. Provide context: Mention your research focus on sprayable formulations
  4. Set constraints: "Limit to 500 words" or "Focus on industrial applications"
  5. Ask for citations: "Cite specific studies when making claims"
  6. Iterate: Refine prompts based on initial responses

🚀 Advanced Prompt Techniques

1. Chain of Thought: Ask AI to "think step by step" about enzymatic mechanisms
2. Few-shot Learning: Provide examples of good enzymatic study analyses
3. Role-playing: "Act as a food safety consultant reviewing this paper"
4. Template Prompts: Create reusable templates for different paper types
5. Multi-step Prompts: Break complex analysis into sequential steps

Integrate AI into Your Research Workflow

Create a seamless workflow combining AI assistants with your existing research tools.

Complete Research Workflow with AI:

1
Discovery: Use ResearchRabbit to find papers on "subtilisin allergen removal"
2
Collection: Save papers to Zotero using Zotero Connector (auto-tagged)
3
Initial Screening: Use Zotero ARIA to generate quick summaries of all papers
4
Deep Analysis: Upload key papers to ChatGPT/Claude for detailed enzymatic analysis
5
Data Extraction: Use specialized prompts to extract experimental parameters
6
Synthesis: Use Perplexity/Copilot to find additional context and recent studies
7
Writing: Use AI to draft literature review sections based on analyzed papers
8
Citation: Use Zotero Word plugin to insert properly formatted citations

Tool-Specific Workflows:

ChatGPT + Zotero Workflow:
  1. Export Zotero references as BibTeX or RIS
  2. Upload to ChatGPT along with PDFs of key papers
  3. Use "Analyze these papers on enzymatic cleaning" prompt
  4. Ask ChatGPT to create an annotated bibliography
  5. Export analysis and save as notes in Zotero
Perplexity for Literature Gaps:
  1. Use Perplexity's "Academic" mode
  2. Search: "research gaps enzymatic allergen removal 2024"
  3. Ask follow-up: "What are the limitations of current sprayable enzyme formulations?"
  4. Use cited papers to find additional references
  5. Save relevant papers to Zotero
Claude for Long Document Analysis:
  1. Upload full PhD theses or lengthy review papers to Claude
  2. Ask: "Extract all information on protease stability in cleaning formulations"
  3. Request: "Create a timeline of enzymatic cleaning technology development"
  4. Use 100K context to analyze multiple papers together

Quality Control and Verification:

🔍 Critical: Verify AI Outputs

AI can generate plausible but incorrect information. Always:
1. Check citations: Verify that cited papers actually exist
2. Cross-reference: Compare AI analysis with your own reading
3. Spot-check data: Verify extracted numbers against original papers
4. Be aware of limitations: AI may miss nuances or context
5. Use multiple AIs: Compare responses from ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity

Time and Cost Optimization:

  • Batch processing: Analyze multiple papers in one session
  • Free tier first: Use free versions for initial screening
  • Specialized tools: Use Perplexity for citations, Claude for long docs
  • Local models: Use Ollama with open-source models for sensitive data
  • Set budgets: Monitor API usage and set monthly limits
