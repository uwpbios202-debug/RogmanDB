Step 2: Summarize Papers

Goal: Use AI to gather the body of work globally.

Basic Search

Prompt 1: Broad Landscape

Please help me find the 10 most recent scholarly articles on Denaturing food Allergens on surfaces.
Regional & Specific

Prompt 2 (Expanded): Global Deep Dives

Use these prompts to ensure you capture international data:

Please find recent scholarly articles (2020-2025) specifically regarding "surface protein denaturation" or "allergen cleaning validation" in food manufacturing contexts within China, India, and Australia. Include specific references to GB 7718 (China) or VITAL 3.0 (Australia/EU) standards.
Compare "enzymatic cleaning" vs "cold plasma" for allergen removal on stainless steel. Find 5 papers that discuss the energy efficiency and cost differences between these methods in Indian or European markets.
Analysis

Prompt 3: The Summary

Summarize these articles. Identify clusters of topics or shifts in focus over time. Note recurring keywords, techniques, and models. Focus heavily on the Discussion and Future Directions sections—highlight any open questions or limitations mentioned.

Step 3: Gaps & Opportunities

Goal: Find the "White Space" where you can contribute.

Prompt 4: Identify Gaps

Identify gaps in the current research landscape on allergen and contact surfaces. Specifically, look for gaps regarding "denaturation verification" (how do they prove it's safe?) and "low-resource implementation" (relevant for India/China/SMEs).

Prompt 5: Brainstorm Experiment

Based on the gaps identified, please help me brainstorm a small, logical experiment I could propose. Frame it as a hypothesis that builds on current work. Suggest how this experiment could be carried out using techniques I can learn (e.g., ELISA, swabbing, basic microscopy).
