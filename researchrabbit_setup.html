Create Your ResearchRabbit Account

ResearchRabbit is a visual literature discovery tool that helps you find related papers and track research trends. It's particularly useful for mapping the enzymatic allergen denaturation field.

Step-by-Step Account Creation:

  1. Visit ResearchRabbit: Go to https://www.researchrabbit.ai
  2. Sign Up: Click "Sign Up" in the top right corner
  3. Choose Registration Method:
    • Recommended: Use your institutional email if available (ends with .edu or similar)
    • Alternatively, use Google account or personal email
  4. Complete Profile:
    • Full Name: RogmanDB (or your preferred name)
    • Research Interests: Include "enzymatic cleaning," "allergen denaturation," "food safety"
    • Institution: University of Wisconsin-Parkside (or your institution)
  5. Verify Email: Check your email for verification link

🎯 Pro Tip for Enzymatic Research

When setting up your profile, mention your specific interest in "protease-based allergen removal" and "sprayable enzyme formulations." This helps ResearchRabbit's recommendation algorithms prioritize relevant papers.

Configure Search Alerts & Keywords

Set up targeted searches and alerts to automatically receive new papers in your field.

Recommended Search Keywords for Enzymatic Research:

🧬 Enzyme-Specific Terms
  • "subtilisin AND allergen"
  • "papain surface cleaning"
  • "protease AND peanut allergen"
  • "enzymatic denaturation food"
🏭 Application Terms
  • "sprayable enzyme foam"
  • "industrial enzyme cleaning"
  • "food contact surface sanitation"
  • "allergen removal validation"
🌍 Regional & Regulatory
  • "GB 7718 allergen control"
  • "VITAL 3.0 enzymatic"
  • "FDA allergen guidance enzyme"
  • "EU food safety protease"

Setting Up Search Alerts:

  1. Go to Discover tab in ResearchRabbit
  2. Enter your search terms (use combinations above)
  3. Apply filters:
    • Publication Date: 2020-2025
    • Document Type: Article, Review
    • Language: English
  4. Click Save Search button
  5. Choose alert frequency: Weekly (recommended)
  6. Select which collections to add new papers to automatically

🔍 Advanced Search Strategy

Create separate searches for:
1. Broad landscape: "enzymatic allergen removal"
2. Specific enzymes: "subtilisin OR papain AND food surface"
3. Validation methods: "ELISA validation enzymatic cleaning"
This ensures comprehensive coverage of your research field.

Connect to Zotero & Verify Setup

Integrating ResearchRabbit with Zotero creates a seamless workflow for managing your enzymatic research papers.

[Image: ResearchRabbit Export Options showing Zotero integration]

Visual showing export button with Zotero option highlighted

Basic Connection Setup:

  1. In ResearchRabbit, go to Settings → Integrations
  2. Find Zotero Integration section
  3. Click Connect to Zotero
  4. Authorize the connection in the pop-up window
  5. Select your Zotero library: Epitope Erase Research
  6. Choose sync preferences:
    • Sync automatically: Yes
    • Include PDFs: Yes (if available)
    • Add tags: Yes (creates tags from ResearchRabbit keywords)

Verification Test:

Perform this test to ensure everything is working:

  1. In ResearchRabbit, find one paper on "enzymatic surface cleaning"
  2. Add it to your Epitope Erase - Enzymatic Denaturation collection
  3. Wait 2-3 minutes for sync
  4. Open Zotero and check your Epitope Erase Research collection
  5. Verify the paper appears with all metadata intact

🔄 Syncing Best Practices

1. Regular Sync Check: Verify weekly that papers added to ResearchRabbit appear in Zotero
2. Tag Consistency: ResearchRabbit tags become Zotero tags - use consistent terminology
3. Backup Strategy: Export your ResearchRabbit collections monthly as JSON backup
